Concept

Linking your days in Nagano, at your own pace.

Time out sightseeing, and time back home to talk it all over — enjoy every part of it at your own pace. A whole-house base right in the city.

The red-brick exterior gives a warm presence on the street. Light through stained glass adds color to the rooms. Natural wood creates an atmosphere where you can simply relax. Unpretentious, and just a little special.

Two Reasons

Close to the station, and a whole house for a group. Both.

Arrive at Nagano Station by Shinkansen and simply walk over — no car needed. There are plenty of restaurants nearby, so after enjoying local Shinshu food and sake, you can walk back home.

7 minutes on foot from the Zenkoji Exit

Convenient for travelers from across Japan and overseas. A base for Zenkoji Temple and for touring northern Nagano, including Togakushi and Obuse.

The whole house, up to 9 guests

At a hotel, a bigger group means separate rooms. Here everyone gathers in the same space — meals, drinks and stories, all together.

Stay Together

After a day in town, your time together begins again.

Pick up a few favorite things on the way back, and raise a glass together once more.

Three generations in one place — a night to stay together a little longer than usual.

A spacious upstairs living room with a large TV. Someone picks tonight's music.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How far is it from Nagano Station?

About a 7-minute walk from the Zenkoji Exit of JR Nagano Station. Look for the Nagano Linden Plaza Hotel as a landmark. You can walk straight from the Shinkansen without a car.

How many guests can stay?

Up to 9 guests. You rent the entire house: 95㎡ over two floors with 3 bedrooms.

Is there parking?

There are several coin-operated parking lots nearby.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 15:00 to 22:00. Check-out is by 10:00.

Is there a kitchen and laundry?

Yes. The second floor has a kitchen with a refrigerator, microwave and electric kettle, and the first floor has a washer-dryer.

How do I book?

Book on the official booking page or on Booking.com. You can also contact us by phone (+81-50-8896-2394) or email (villasenshin0713@gmail.com).

Do you speak English?

Yes. We can assist in Japanese and English.

Is smoking allowed?

No. The entire house is non-smoking.
